# Design Acceptance — Spec 061

The patient workspace follows `DESIGN.md` by using a compact patient header, whitespace/separators over nested cards, keyboard-reachable segment tabs, dense timeline/lab rows, visible provenance, explicit missing/unsupported states, and restrained status color paired with text.

Acceptance points:
- patient identity/condition/coverage state is visible without inventing missing facts;
- longitudinal events expose source/provenance state;
- labs preserve value/unit/coverage labels;
- medication/document/care-plan limitations are shown in dedicated honesty panels;
- primary tabs use native focus semantics and visible focus treatment;
- synthetic-demo labeling is persistent;
- no chatbot-first patient shell or authority-changing shortcut was introduced.

This is engineering/design acceptance only. It is not final screen-reader, WCAG, localization, or qualified-hardware product evidence; Spec 067 owns final qualification.
