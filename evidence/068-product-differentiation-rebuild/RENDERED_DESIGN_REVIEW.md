## Home verdict

PASS for the Spec 068 product-bar objective.

The surface reads as a clinical workspace rather than an analytics/admin dashboard. Patient context, source/identity/authority state, model truth, clinical flow, and review work dominate the hierarchy. Fake KPI tiles are absent.
## Models verdict

PASS for truthful productization of model/runtime state.

The Model Center is not a developer log. It exposes task, runtime, device boundary, trust/provenance posture, benchmark evidence, and admission state. Hugging Face is explicitly presented as a model source rather than an authority plane.

## Evidence verdict

PASS for evidence-backed competitor positioning.

The Evidence Center makes the pinned OpenMed baseline visible and separates MedScale and OpenMed evidence per capability. `PROVEN ADVANTAGE` is reserved for bound evidence; gaps remain visible rather than being hidden behind marketing copy.

## Brand verdict

PASS for the Spec 068 identity objective, pending repository CI qualification.

The rendered product uses the standalone MedScale core mark, Geist typography on the development host, an obsidian/ice/signal-blue system, and restrained semantic state colors. The rejected Cohere-like multicolor identity is absent.
