- GitHub-hosted CI runners are measurement hosts, not declared qualified release hardware.
- Cold launch covers the current model-free desktop scaffold plus doctor initialization, not a future final shell.

- `perf_budgets_attained_on_qualified_hardware` remains a missing release evidence class.
- `RELEASE_READY=false` remains unchanged.
- No real PHI, signing credentials, production effects, or MESC mutation are involved.
