# Spec 072 — Product Requalification

**Status**: CLOSED_CANONICAL
**Base**: `e2031bdef918980cc184aed811f381e41f5dfb8b`

## Goal

Requalify the rebuilt native MedScale Desktop+CLI product after Specs 068–071, without turning repository-owned engineering evidence into release, privacy, accessibility, clinical, or comparative claims that still require external measurement or authority.

## Required outcomes

1. Requalify the complete final native route inventory, including the post-Spec-067 `Models` and `Evidence` surfaces, with no future-slice placeholder or invented patient/risk state.
2. Requalify Model Center truth boundaries: Core-owned Pack authority, session-local inventory, qualification-reference separation, local signed-Pack admission, no product online acquisition, and no real-PHI promotion.
3. Requalify Evidence Center claim discipline: pinned OpenMed identity, 39/39 ledger accounting, explicit limitations, no unmanifested parity/surpass/privacy-superiority/runtime-winner verdict.
4. Requalify accessibility engineering invariants across the rebuilt product and publish a new external action packet that explicitly covers Models and Evidence in addition to the earlier final routes. No WCAG/assistive-technology conformance claim is permitted without external observations.
5. Requalify performance coverage and publish a new qualified-hardware action packet that explicitly includes Models and Evidence route/interaction measurements. Hosted CI/development-host results remain feasibility evidence only.
6. Reaudit the release doctor and living external-gate map so every remaining release evidence class is external-only and exactly actionable; MESC is a separate project and must be absent from MedScale gates, residuals, completion axes, and execution scope.
7. Preserve portable-package, SBOM, branch-protection, required-check, model-runtime, and OpenMed claim-honesty regressions through local qualification and exact-head CI.
8. Complete Rust 1.88, formatting, Clippy, workspace/focused tests, Desktop smoke/perf probes, exact-range review, exact-head CI, protected merge, and post-main verification before closure.
9. After 072 closure, hand off to final repository-implementation closure. Do not set `RELEASE_READY`, `PRIVATE_DATA_READY`, `MULTI_CLIENT_RELEASE_READY`, `REAL_PHI_AUTHORIZED`, WCAG conformance, qualified-hardware attainment, or signing/notarization claims without their independent evidence.

## Explicit non-goals

- No new product feature family, mobile implementation, cloud authority plane, or online model acquisition.
- No real PHI, production partner credentials, signing secrets, or external legal decisions.
- No work in the separate MESC project. Historical MedScale Specs 012/036 remain interoperability provenance only and do not create current MedScale scope.
- No attempt to close external accessibility, hardware-performance, signing/notarization, private-data, or platform-qualified sandbox gates from repository evidence alone.
