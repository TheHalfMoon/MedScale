## Working novelty hypothesis

The current literature supports provenance, logging, evidence grading, operational safety, and auditable inference as established research directions. The hypothesis to test is narrower: **whether MedScale's combination of Core authority separation, executable provenance-bearing artifacts, explicit subsystem failure/unknown semantics, fail-closed external boundaries, and recovery qualification constitutes a distinct systems contribution when evaluated together at an exact revision.**

This hypothesis remains falsifiable. New primary sources may narrow or eliminate individual novelty claims.
