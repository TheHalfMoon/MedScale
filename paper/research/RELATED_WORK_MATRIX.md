## Working novelty hypothesis after September 2026 screening

The literature establishes clear prior art for provenance, evidence ledgers, deterministic evidence gates, non-self-certifying generators, logging, abstention/selective autonomy, on-premise medical AI, FHIR-facing audit evidence, adversarial evidence validation, and exact-release reproduction. MedScale must not claim novelty for these ideas in isolation.

The remaining falsifiable hypothesis is systems-level: **whether one local-first research architecture can enforce and jointly evaluate (1) Core-mediated authority separation across source/derived/model/human/action/effect planes, (2) provenance-bound artifacts, (3) explicit subsystem unknown/failure semantics, (4) fail-closed external boundaries, and (5) application-state backup/restore/restart qualification at an exact revision.**

New primary sources may narrow or eliminate individual parts of this hypothesis. A matrix cell may move from `NOT_REPORTED` only after supporting primary evidence is recorded.
