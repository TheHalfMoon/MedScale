\section{Conclusion}

MedScale studies a systems-level complement to model-centric medical AI evaluation: making authority, provenance, epistemic state, recovery, and external effects explicit and testable. The frozen implementation provides a concrete substrate for this question, including a Core authority path, digest-verifiable source contracts, explicit unknown evidence state, fail-closed network boundaries, and whole-platform synthetic qualification with recorded defect discovery and repair. The dedicated paper campaign will determine how these properties behave under controlled provenance mutations, adversarial faults, recovery, and scale. Until those experiments are complete, quantitative superiority and clinical-safety claims are intentionally withheld.
