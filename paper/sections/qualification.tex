\section{Qualification Methodology}

MedScale's repository process binds promoted work to exact revisions, deterministic qualification, exact-head CI, merge evidence, and post-main verification. The whole-platform qualification campaign (Spec 092) is the principal pre-existing integrated evidence source for this paper.

At the frozen snapshot, the Spec 092 matrix classifies 35 areas using a conservative status vocabulary rather than treating all implemented behavior as proven. The integrated campaign exercises Research OS planes in one synthetic vault across backup, restore, consistency verification, and restart. Exact-head and post-merge CI were green on hosted Linux, Windows, and macOS jobs for the qualified synthetic profile.

Two failures discovered during integrated qualification are scientifically important because they demonstrate that the methodology was capable of falsifying expected behavior. One defect lost ID-sequence state across restore, creating a post-restore collision/refusal risk; it was repaired with a restore regression test. A second failure arose from nondeterministic privacy leak checking when a short value collided with substrings of digests/identifiers/timestamps; the failed run was retained as evidence, the matcher was made boundary-aware, and a regression test was added. Neither defect is presented as a clinical event.

The new paper campaign extends this repository qualification with controlled provenance mutations, a structured failure corpus, repeated recovery experiments, and measurements intended specifically for publication.
