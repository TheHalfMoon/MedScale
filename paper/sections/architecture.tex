\section{MedScale Architecture}

MedScale is organized around a Rust-owned Core authority boundary shared by admitted client surfaces. Research OS planes---including project/artifact management, data, collaboration, agents, model execution, privacy controls, evidence/research workflows, analytics, compute, R workspace metadata, extensions, institutional adapters, and federation---are composed around this boundary rather than granted independent authority over canonical state.

The architecture deliberately distinguishes useful local operation from network-dependent operation. In the frozen qualification profile, the whole-platform synthetic campaign runs without requiring an account, remote model, or network fallback. Network institutional transport and live partner integrations remain external gates rather than being simulated into a production-readiness claim.
