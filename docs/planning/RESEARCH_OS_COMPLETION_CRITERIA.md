# MedScale Research OS Planning Completion Criteria

This planning packet is considered structurally complete when it contains:

- product vision and target users;
- architecture and authority boundaries;
- product map;
- AudioFlow plan;
- source adoption strategy and curated ledger;
- security/threat/scale framing;
- acceptance and Definition-of-Ready rules;
- migration strategy;
- dependency-ordered candidate roadmap;
- open questions that intentionally remain evidence-gated;
- real lab/adoption journeys;
- explicit statement that canonical implementation authority is unchanged.

Structural completeness does not mean technical choices are proven or the Research OS is implemented.
