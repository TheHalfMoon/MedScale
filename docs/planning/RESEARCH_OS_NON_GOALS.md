# MedScale Research OS Non-Goals

**Status:** Planning candidate.

The Research OS program is not an instruction to build every adjacent product category.

Near-term and structural non-goals include:

- replacing specialized scientific instruments or vendor control software;
- becoming a full hospital EHR/ERP;
- becoming a general-purpose Slack/GitHub/Notion replacement unrelated to research work;
- becoming a public social network;
- treating multi-agent debate as scientific validation;
- unrestricted shell/browser access to sensitive stores;
- automatic cloud synchronization;
- automatic upload of recordings or transcripts;
- voice cloning without explicit rights/permission;
- hiding complex external services behind a misleading local-only label;
- copying donor products wholesale to accelerate feature count;
- forcing every scientific domain into Core before a Pack demonstrates demand.

The product should integrate with specialized systems and own the research intelligence/provenance/workflow layer where that creates clear value.
