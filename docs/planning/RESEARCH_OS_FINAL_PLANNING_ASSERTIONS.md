# MedScale Research OS Final Planning Assertions

These assertions summarize the planning packet without promoting implementation.

1. MedScale scales to laboratories and institutions only if Project/Artifact/Capability semantics are stabilized before adding feature islands.
2. MedAgent becomes a governed intelligence workbench; multi-model Fleet comparison reports observable evidence and uncertainty rather than winner scores.
3. Privacy Gate sits across all model, Browse, export, Hub, Compute, connector and adapter boundaries rather than existing as one de-identification screen.
4. Governed Browse is an independently owned capability because web research introduces egress, credential, redirect/SSRF, hostile-content, download-quarantine and evidence-provenance risks that cannot be hidden inside MedAgent.
5. AudioFlow deserves first-class status because capture, STT, diarization, voice control, meetings, TTS and audio evidence share governance/runtime concerns.
6. Buzz and VoiceStudio are high-value donors, but both are consumed selectively behind MedScale-owned contracts and exact provenance/rights/security review.
7. DataFusion/Arrow is a strong qualification target for native analytics; full BI/RAG platforms remain optional adapters unless evidence proves otherwise.
8. Hub and Compute are scale mechanisms: Hub scales collaboration; Compute scales execution. Neither widens Core authority by default.
9. Research Packs scale scientific domains without bloating Core or allowing arbitrary trusted-process extensions.
10. Personal/offline operation remains a valid product topology at every generation.
11. The first future implementation unit is the smallest Project/Artifact foundation required by the rest of the graph, after live canonical reconciliation.
12. The planning packet now has explicit implementation contracts, repository ownership mapping, safe decision defaults, verification campaigns and a recorded gap-closure review.
