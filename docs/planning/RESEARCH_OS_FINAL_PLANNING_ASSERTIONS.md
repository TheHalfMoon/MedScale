# MedScale Research OS Final Planning Assertions

These assertions summarize the planning direction without promoting implementation:

1. MedScale can scale to laboratories and institutions only if Project/Artifact/Capability semantics are stabilized before adding feature islands.
2. MedAgent should become a governed intelligence workbench, with multi-model Fleet comparison built on observable evidence rather than winner scores.
3. Privacy Gate must sit across all egress/sharing/worker boundaries, not exist as a standalone de-identification screen.
4. AudioFlow deserves first-class status because capture, STT, diarization, voice control, meetings, TTS and audio evidence share governance/runtime concerns.
5. Buzz and VoiceStudio are high-value donors, but both should be consumed selectively behind MedScale-owned contracts.
6. DataFusion/Arrow is a strong qualification target for native analytics; full BI/RAG platforms remain optional adapters unless evidence proves otherwise.
7. Hub and Compute are the scale mechanisms: Hub scales collaboration; Compute scales execution. Neither should widen Core authority by default.
8. Research Packs are the scale mechanism for scientific domains.
9. Personal/offline operation remains a product topology at every generation.
10. The first future implementation unit should be the smallest Project/Artifact foundation required by the rest of the graph, after live canonical reconciliation.
