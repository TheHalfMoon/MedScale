# MedScale ScaleFold Visual Language

**Status:** FOUNDER_APPROVED_HANDOFF — Spec 093

## Purpose

The ScaleFold M is not a badge placed on top of MedScale. It is the generative rule behind the broader visual language.

The goal is recognizability without constant logo repetition. A MedScale surface should still feel related to MedScale when the standalone mark is hidden.

ScaleFold Language is original MedScale geometry. Abridge, JetBrains, Cohere, Pentagram work, and other references are craft research only; their proprietary marks, custom type, waveforms, layouts, and shape systems must not be copied.

## Core primitives

The language is built from five recurring ideas:
- **Pillar** — a stable vertical measure representing a source, artifact, model lane, or unit of work.
- **Fold** — the decisive diagonal transition used in the ScaleFold M.
- **Step** — a measured change in height or depth that creates progression without implying quality.
- **Gap** — intentional negative space that keeps parallel structures legible.
- **Rhythm** — repetition with controlled variation rather than decorative randomness.
## ScaleFold Supergraphics

Supergraphics are enlarged derivatives of the mark geometry, not enlarged copies of the full logo.

Allowed treatments:
- crop one fold aggressively at page edges;
- repeat two or three pillar/fold units as a quiet rhythm;
- use a single oversized diagonal transition to divide editorial space;
- use low-opacity geometry behind an intro, empty state, event graphic, or report cover;
- let geometry extend beyond the viewport so it feels architectural rather than sticker-like.

Supergraphics must never:
- mimic Abridge waveform typography or masonry forms;
- become a decorative gradient on every product card;
- encode clinical severity, model quality, evidence confidence, or authority;
- reduce text contrast or compete with primary work.

In Work mode, supergraphics should be absent or nearly invisible. In Brand mode, they may become a dominant compositional device.

## ScaleFold Display

ScaleFold Display is a **treatment**, not a new typeface.

Instrument Sans remains the canonical display/product family. For a small number of hero words, launch moments, event titles, or brand lockups, V0 may create custom outlined display compositions derived from ScaleFold rules:
- measured 45-degree cuts;
- stepped baselines or cap-height transitions;
- controlled gaps;
- repeated vertical rhythm;
- asymmetric endings related to the shorter closing pillar.

Do not alter normal UI text, body copy, navigation, tables, evidence labels, or the canonical MedScale wordmark.
Custom display treatment must remain legible before it becomes clever. Never distort text merely to resemble the M.

## Pattern grammar

Patterns may be generated from ScaleFold primitives using:
- regular repetition;
- alternating pillar heights;
- positive/negative cutouts;
- bounded horizontal progression;
- sparse field placement with generous negative space.

Prefer one pattern rule per composition. Avoid wallpaper density.

Pattern color uses either:
- monochrome ink/white;
- a single solid brand accent;
- the MedScale Spectrum in a bounded brand-only context.

## Product integration

ScaleFold influence in product UI should be subtle and structural:
- selected-section markers may borrow the fold angle;
- comparison lanes may use measured stepped headers;
- split-pane dividers may use a tiny fold notch as an optional affordance;
- empty states may use a cropped monochrome fold;
- research/report covers may use full supergraphics;
- evidence and artifact connectors may use disciplined orthogonal-plus-diagonal geometry.

Do not paste miniature M shapes into every component. The system should be recognized through proportion and rhythm, not repeated logos.
## Motion grammar

Brand motion may assemble the mark from pillars and folds, then release into the product workspace.

Preferred sequence:
1. pillars establish stable rhythm;
2. folds connect and resolve into the ScaleFold M;
3. the full spectrum appears only at the identity moment;
4. geometry recedes as the workspace enters Work mode.

A second valid sequence is **Source → Evidence → Action**, where three neutral geometric units progressively connect before the mark resolves. This is a metaphor for workflow only; it must not imply clinical authority or correctness.

Reference timing:
- micro fold/selection response: 100–180 ms;
- pane or section geometry transition: 160–240 ms;
- brand assembly: 450–800 ms;
- full intro sequence: no more than actual initialization time and preferably under 1200 ms.

Reduced-motion mode uses a static final composition or opacity-only transition.

No perpetual loops, particle fields, pulsing gradients, or waveform imitation.

## Recognition test

A ScaleFold composition passes only if:
- it feels related to MedScale without showing the complete logo;
- it remains useful when rendered in monochrome;
- it survives both light and dark themes;
- it does not resemble a competitor's signature device;
- it does not compromise product clarity or evidence semantics.

The logo is one expression of the system. The system is larger than the logo.
