# MedScale Icon System

**Status:** CANONICAL_SPEC_093

## Purpose

MedScale icons should be recognizable before they are decorative. Their job is to accelerate navigation and scanning across dense research and clinical workflows.

## Native product grammar

- 24×24 source grid for navigation icons;
- approximately 1.7–1.8 px optical stroke at source size;
- rounded caps and joins;
- simple silhouettes with one primary idea per icon;
- avoid tiny internal detail that disappears at 16–20 px;
- do not place every icon inside a colored square;
- active state comes from surrounding control state, not by recoloring the meaning of the icon;
- text/accessibility labels remain semantic authority.

## Brand/feature icon grammar

Marketing, onboarding, and high-level feature callouts may use a soft-tint tile or a single spectrum accent, but only when it improves grouping or recognition. Never use a rainbow set merely to decorate a dashboard.

## Distinctiveness

MedScale should prefer original combinations and proportions over direct copies of familiar icon packs. Generic concepts such as search, settings, document, person, database, and shield may use conventional metaphors, but the line treatment and optical balance remain consistent with the MedScale family.

## Forbidden motifs

Do not make the product identity depend on:
- medical crosses;
- ECG/heartbeat traces;
- brains or neural-network clichés;
- sparkle-only AI icons;
- stethoscope marks;
- mascots;
- vendor logos.
