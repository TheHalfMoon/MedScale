# MedScale Identity Reference Research — 2026-09-17

**Status:** REFERENCE_ONLY — NOT VISUAL AUTHORITY

The founder asked MedScale to study the craft of JetBrains, Abridge, and Cohere while remaining unmistakably MedScale. This document records transferable principles only. No competitor logo, icon, illustration, layout, proprietary font, or branded asset is copied.

## JetBrains

Primary references:
- https://www.jetbrains.com/
- https://www.jetbrains.com/company/brand/
- https://www.jetbrains.com/help/idea/new-ui.html

Useful principles:
- strong recognition comes from a compact mark plus a repeatable high-energy color signature;
- the 2026 IDE UI explicitly reduces visual complexity and progressively discloses advanced controls;
- interface theme and editor/content color are separate systems rather than one decorative palette;
- icons prioritize distinguishable silhouettes, legibility, and balance;
- high-saturation brand color works best when product chrome remains controlled.

Boundary for MedScale: JetBrains brand assets are proprietary and its public guidelines prohibit imitation. MedScale therefore uses an independently drawn mark, independently selected color values, and a healthcare/research-specific product grammar.

## Abridge

Primary references:
- https://www.abridge.com/
- https://www.abridge.com/product
- https://www.abridge.com/blog/building-trusted-healthcare-ai

Useful principles:
- human work remains the visual and product center; technology recedes behind the clinical task;
- workflow drives layout and interaction rather than feature inventory;
- transparency and source linkage are trust features, not compliance footnotes;
- editorial warmth, generous whitespace, and confident type can make enterprise healthcare feel human without becoming playful;
- marketing can carry more personality than the clinician-facing work surface.

Boundary for MedScale: no Abridge illustration, color recipe, page composition, icon, or copy pattern is reused. MedScale remains research/evidence-first rather than ambient-documentation-first.

## Cohere

Primary references:
- https://cohere.com/
- https://cohere.com/newsroom
- https://cohere.com/blog/designing-ai-assistants-that-workers-will-love-to-use

Useful principles:
- large declarative headings establish hierarchy with very little ornament;
- neutral light surfaces let occasional graphic systems and color moments carry brand recognition;
- enterprise security, control, and deployment boundaries are communicated as product value rather than hidden infrastructure;
- simple cards and editorial spacing can coexist with technically dense material.

Boundary for MedScale: no Cohere logo construction, graphic shape library, illustration, typography asset, or page structure is copied.

## MedScale synthesis

MedScale adopts its own combination:
1. **Recognizable signal:** the ScaleFold M and MedScale Spectrum own the brand moment.
2. **Quiet work surfaces:** research and clinical work use neutral, high-contrast light/dark surfaces.
3. **Evidence-native trust:** provenance, source state, uncertainty, and authority remain visually near consequential output.
4. **Progressive density:** simple entry points reveal technical depth without flattening it.
5. **Human technicality:** typography is confident and readable; long-form evidence may use serif rhythm while controls remain sans.
6. **Color discipline:** gradient is identity; semantic color is state. They are never interchangeable.
