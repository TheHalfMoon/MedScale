# MedScale Voice and Copy

**Status:** CANONICAL_DRAFT_SPEC_068

## 1. Voice

MedScale speaks like a precise clinical-technical colleague: concise, calm, specific, and explicit about uncertainty.

Preferred qualities:
- direct over promotional;
- concrete over abstract;
- evidence-linked over confident-sounding;
- useful over clever;
- human without forced friendliness.

## 2. Core vocabulary

Prefer:
- evidence
- source
- provenance
- review
- admitted / not admitted
- supported / unsupported
- unresolved
- reconcile
- local
- authority
- proposal
- qualified / unqualified

These words encode product truth and should remain consistent across Desktop, CLI, docs, and diagnostics.
## 3. Avoid

Do not use:
- revolutionary
- game-changing
- magical
- effortless
- smarter healthcare
- next-generation AI
- powered by AI as filler
- best-in-class without comparative evidence
- seamless when a real boundary or handoff exists

Do not hide uncertainty behind softer language. If a runtime is not admitted, say so. If evidence is incomplete, say what is missing.

## 4. Product copy patterns

Good:
- `Production inference not admitted.`
- `Review required before action.`
- `Source conflict unresolved.`
- `Evidence present; authority unchanged.`
- `Unknown delivery state. Reconcile before retry.`
- `Model source verified; benchmark not yet qualified.`

Avoid:
- `AI-powered insights ready.`
- `Everything looks great.`
- `Smart recommendations.`
- `Instantly transform care.`
- `MedScale beats OpenMed.`

## 5. Buttons and actions

Use verb + object labels when possible: `Review evidence`, `Open patient`, `Inspect model`, `Reconcile status`, `Prepare action`.

Avoid vague controls such as `Continue`, `Go`, `Magic`, `Generate` when the actual operation can be named.
